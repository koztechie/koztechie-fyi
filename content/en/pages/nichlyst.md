NICHLYST is an atmospheric narrative game for Android about memory, listening, and preserving the truth. Every day, people come to you with their accounts: your role is to document what they went through and protect their words from corruption.

Originally written in Ukrainian, the game also features a complete English translation. No intrusive ads, timers, or subscriptions — just a single one-time purchase.
