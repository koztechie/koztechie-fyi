NICHLYST is a story-driven game for Android about working with memory and preserving testimonies. Every day, people come to you with their stories: your job is to listen to them, record their testimonies, and protect them from decay.

The game is made in Ukrainian and fully localized in English. No intrusive ads or subscriptions — just a single one-time purchase.
