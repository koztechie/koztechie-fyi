A narrative Android game about collecting and preserving testimonies. Every decision has consequences.

Visitors come to you every day with testimonies: you record them and keep them from decaying.

The game is available in Ukrainian and English. Ukrainian is the primary language.

[**Download NICHLYST on Google Play →**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.koztechie.nichlyst)
