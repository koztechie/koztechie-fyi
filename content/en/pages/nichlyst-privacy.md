NICHLYST is an Android game by koztechie. The game does not collect your name, email address, or location to identify you. Third-party SDKs used for push notifications, purchases, and app stability may process technical data.

## Third-party SDKs and technical data

- **OneSignal** delivers narrative push notifications. It may process an anonymous device identifier (Player ID), device model, operating system version, and language settings.
- [**RevenueCat**](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) validates the in-app purchase using an anonymous app user ID and purchase information. The game offers only one one-time purchase to unlock the full version; there are no subscriptions. RevenueCat does not store payment card details.
- **Sentry** helps identify crashes. It may process technical device logs, operating system version, and error stack traces.

This data is used to deliver push notifications, validate the purchase, and fix software errors. SDK data is transmitted over encrypted connections; each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Save data and payments

Game progress is stored locally in the app's storage on your device and is not uploaded to the developer's servers. Uninstalling the app may delete the local save.

Google Play Billing processes payments. Google handles financial transactions; the developer does not have access to payment details.

## Children

The game is rated 16+ and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal data.

## Your rights and how to contact us

You can disable push notifications in your device settings or uninstall the game. You can also request access to or deletion of technical data. To help locate records held by third-party SDKs, include an available in-game or SDK identifier in your request; do not send payment credentials. Requests about payment data and transactions should be directed to Google Play.

Send privacy questions and requests through the <a href="/en/contact/">contact page</a>.
