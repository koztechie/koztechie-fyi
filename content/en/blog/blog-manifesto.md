This blog is a place for my thoughts and notes. Project write-ups and experiments go to the Practice section. I don't post news — it gets old too fast, whereas a good idea can stay relevant for years.

## Posting schedule

I aim for a maximum of one post per week, but I might skip some. There's no strict schedule: I only write when I actually have something to say, not just to keep the blog active.

## Titles

Titles will be short and to the point. No clickbait, false intrigue, or forced numbers.

## No cross-posting

Blog posts stay in the blog. Case studies belong in the Practice section. I won't duplicate the same text across sections, though I might link between them.

## What won't be here

You won't find vanity metrics (like posts per month), mandatory weekly columns, share buttons, or likes.

Instead, every post simply gets a permanent, sequential number, starting from №1.
